About Delta

A local contractor with a modern approach.

Delta Multiholding Solution (DMS) is a Tanzanian company specialised in civil and building construction, considered one of the most highly developed and modernised contracting companies in its field.

Who we are

Quality, on budget
and on time.

DMS is a construction company with a reputation for delivering projects of the highest quality, within the budget and time stipulated in the contract agreement. We execute our projects with the highest ethics and mobilise quickly.

Our strength lies in our experience and our ability to deliver when it counts. We are registered by the Contractors Registration Board (CRB) as a Class V contractor for both Building and Civil works.

Our background

Advanced facilities for every kind of client.

Our success is driven by our promise to deliver quality projects safely, on time and within budget. We are known for building advanced facilities for individual, corporate, institutional and government clients.

Many of our clients turn to us for our ability to implement innovative project management techniques, and as a reliable, knowledge-driven partner for complex construction projects. Whether it’s restoration, site preparation, a new facility or a renovation, we have the experience and personnel to deliver the highest quality construction on schedule and within budget.

Our network of local offices gives clients the dual advantage of a strong local presence and broad geographical reach.

Our policies

How we work,
on and off site.

Health & safety

Protecting the health and safety of our employees, subcontractors and the public is fundamental to our business success. We are committed to safety above all else, and this commitment is central to everything we do. No task is so important that we cannot find a safe way to do it.

Through this philosophy, we believe an injury- and incident-free workplace is an achievable goal.

Environment

We are committed to having a minimal impact on the environment, because environmental protection is an integral part of our business, our success and our future. Our goal is to continually improve our operations by setting, reviewing and updating our environmental performance.

Empowerment

Delta Multiholding Solution is committed to developing and maintaining substantial opportunity and participation for qualified and capable minority, women and disadvantaged subcontractors and suppliers wherever possible.

Corporate social responsibility

Giving back to the communities in which we work is a commitment we take to heart. We get involved by donating our time, money, materials and expertise, and we organise group opportunities with different causes and charities by encouraging our employees to volunteer in the community.
